Frequently Asked Questions

How do you handle hosting, maintenance, and third-party costs?
While we engineer and deploy your systems, the client directly incurs the costs for all third-party subscriptions. This includes server hosting tiers (e.g., AWS, Vercel), domain names, database storage, and usage-based APIs like AI tokens. We provision these accounts directly under your company's billing profile to ensure you retain full ownership and complete transparency over your operational overhead.
Who owns the intellectual property and code?
You do. Upon final payment of the project, full intellectual property rights, repository access, and production codebase ownership are transferred to your organization.
Can you integrate with our existing software (CRMs, ERPs)?
Yes. We routinely build custom API bridges to connect new applications with existing operational tools like Salesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, AWS, and proprietary legacy databases.
What happens after the project is launched?
We offer SLA (Service Level Agreement) support tiers. Post-launch, we monitor system uptime, handle bug regressions, and are available to scope and deploy iterative feature expansions as your business scales.
How do you handle data security?
We implement industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit (AES-256 and TLS 1.3). Database access is strictly governed by least-privilege IAM roles, and we never store sensitive operational data on intermediary proxy servers.
What is the typical project timeline?
Timelines vary strictly based on scope. Standard frontend marketing websites generally take 2-4 weeks, while complex full-stack internal dashboards and user portals average 6-12 weeks from scoping to deployment.
